# Brand identity

The public site is not live. This document is the Stage 2 brand for the local
preview and for Canva comps. It does not spend money or publish the site.

## Name

Agents Blog

## Tagline

Clear writing for people who build AI agents.

## Audience signal

AI engineers, researchers, and tech leads.

## Voice

Essay-first, specific, and calm. Prefer one idea treated carefully over a news
roundup. Avoid product marketing chrome, emoji-led section headers, and a
second accent palette.

## Colour

| Role | Light | Dark |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Page | `#f7f4ef` background, `#1c1915` text | `#1c1915` background, `#f7f4ef` text |
| Link | Same as text, underlined | Same as text, underlined |
| Focus ring | `2px solid #1c1915` with `2px` offset on `#f7f4ef` | `2px solid #f7f4ef` with `2px` offset on `#1c1915` |

Do not introduce a purple gradient, terracotta accent, or a second brand
palette until a design review says so.

## Typography

Reading and brand display use Georgia (Times New Roman fallback). There is no
separate display face for Stage 2. Keep the reading measure near `40rem`.

## Home hierarchy

On `/` (and the empty index), the first viewport is:

1. Global nav
2. Brand name as the sole `h1`: Agents Blog
3. Tagline under the brand name
4. Optional pillar browse labels (Foundations, Building, Evaluation, Safety,
   Operations) — editorial filters, not routes
5. Post list (title, date, one-line summary)

## Logo

Free Canva wordmark (no paid assets): see [Canva designs](#canva-designs).
The mark is the words “Agents Blog” in a serif weight on the page background.
A paid custom logo or typeface still waits on Venkat’s approval before spend.

## Cover template

Open Graph / social cover composition (`1200×630` landscape):

1. Brand name: Agents Blog
2. One post title
3. One short summary line
4. Optional tag names under the summary

Background `#f7f4ef`, text `#1c1915`, Georgia. No photo required.
