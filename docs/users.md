# For readers

The public site is not live yet.

This page is for people who want to read Agents Blog. It says what you can do now, and what you will be able to do when the site opens.

## What you can do today

The public blog is not live. There is no domain, no posts, and no signup form yet. A local preview can show search, an RSS feed, a dark theme, and draft policy pages. Those pages are not the public site. You cannot join a mailing list, because signup is not connected.

## Reading posts

When the site opens, you will be able to open a post and read it. The posts will be about AI agents. You will be able to follow tags, and to read a series of posts that belong together. You will be able to search for a topic. You will be able to follow new posts with RSS. The pages will include a dark mode, so you can read with a darker screen if you prefer it.

## Newsletter

We plan a double opt-in newsletter. You will subscribe with your email address. You will then confirm that email before you are on the list. Each message will let you unsubscribe in one click.

## Privacy

We will not set non-essential cookies before you give consent. The site will follow UK data protection law (UK GDPR) and the UK cookie rules (PECR). Policies are not published yet.

## Accessibility

We aim for pages that meet WCAG 2.2 AA. You should be able to move through the site with a keyboard. Text should have enough contrast to read. Images should have alt text that says what they show. The site is not audited yet.

## Who to contact

The project owner is Venkat. This page does not list an email address. A way to write to the project will be published with the site.
