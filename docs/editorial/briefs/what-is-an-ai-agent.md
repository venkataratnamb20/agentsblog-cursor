## Claim

An agent is a goal-directed loop that chooses tools; a workflow is a fixed
graph; a chatbot is turn-based chat without tool autonomy.

## Promise

Give the reader a crisp, usable distinction they can apply when naming a
system in design docs, reviews, or architecture discussions: agent vs
workflow vs chatbot.

## Success signal

A tech lead can classify a proposed system into one of the three categories
from the claim without rereading the post, and can say why the other two
do not fit.
